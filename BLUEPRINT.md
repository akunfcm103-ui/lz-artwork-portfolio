# LZ-ArtWork Freelance Commission Blueprint

## 1. Positioning

LZ-ArtWork menawarkan custom digital art dengan gaya visual yang jelas, proses terarah, komunikasi personal, dan batas revisi yang transparan.

Target awal:
- Individu yang ingin character/personal art.
- Penulis atau creator yang membutuhkan visual karakter.
- Small business yang membutuhkan ilustrasi visual.

Jangan menargetkan semua orang sekaligus. Pilih satu audience utama untuk tiap campaign.

## 2. Offer structure

Keep it simple:
- Base service: jenis commission dan output.
- Add-ons: character tambahan, background, commercial usage, deadline khusus jika tersedia.
- Revisi: mengikuti aturan bisnis yang sudah ditetapkan.
- Quote: `PRICING_RULE_REQUIRED` jika rule belum dipilih untuk kasus tersebut.

Jangan membuat nominal harga baru dari tebakan.

## 3. Customer journey

1. Discovery: portfolio, process content, testimonial.
2. Inquiry: client mengirim brief, reference, deadline, usage.
3. Qualification: cek scope, kapasitas, risiko, dan kecocokan.
4. Proposal/quote: detail output, timeline, revisi, usage, harga sesuai rule.
5. Owner approval: kamu menyetujui commission dan terms.
6. Payment state: simulasi atau instruksi setelah approval; tidak ada payment action otomatis.
7. Production: sketch → progress → final.
8. Revision: track request dan jumlah revisi.
9. Final approval: client/owner approval sebelum delivery.
10. Delivery: file final dikirim sesuai agreed format.
11. Retention: feedback, client history, referral request dengan izin.

## 4. Marketing system

### Product marketing
- Record target audience, problem, desired outcome, differentiation, proof.
- Use client language from real inquiries.

### Copywriting
- Bio: siapa kamu + jenis art + untuk siapa + CTA.
- Portfolio: visual first, caption explains problem/outcome/process.
- CTA: one clear next action, e.g. `Kirim brief commission`.

### Offers
- Base package + limited add-ons.
- Avoid confusing tier explosion.
- Scarcity only when real: available slots/capacity.

### CRO
Review manually before building experiments:
- Value proposition visible.
- Portfolio examples relevant.
- Price/process expectations clear.
- CTA easy to find.
- Trust signals authentic.

### Social/content
Content pillars:
- Finished artwork.
- WIP/process/timelapse.
- Education/behind the scenes.
- Client result/testimonial with permission.
- Slot availability/commission announcement.

Quality and consistency beat daily posting.

### Retention
- Delivery follow-up.
- Ask for feedback.
- Record preferences and past scope.
- Referral request only when appropriate.

## 5. Metrics for early stage

Track weekly:
- Qualified inquiries.
- Response time.
- Quote acceptance rate.
- Completed commissions.
- Average turnaround.
- Revision count.
- Repeat/referral inquiries.

Do not optimize vanity metrics before basic inquiry-to-completion flow works.

## 6. Approval and safety gates

JARVIS may draft, classify, track, and recommend.

Owner approval required for:
- Final pricing.
- Scope changes/additional work.
- Commission acceptance.
- Agreements/terms.
- Payment instructions or confirmation.
- External client communication.
- Public posting/campaign publication.
- Refund, discount, or exception.

No real payment, client communication, or permanent business write during simulation.

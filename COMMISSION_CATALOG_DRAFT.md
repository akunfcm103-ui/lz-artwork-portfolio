## Quote rules

1. Use only the canonical rules above.
2. Never invent a price for unsupported, ambiguous, or out-of-scope work.
3. Mark unresolved pricing as `NEEDS_RYA_REVIEW`.
4. Price is not final until Rya approves it.
5. Quotation or discussion does not equal commission acceptance.
6. DP 50% follows the approved final price and must be verified by Rya.
7. Accepted payment methods: QRIS and DANA.
